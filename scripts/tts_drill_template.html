How to review
  1. Press Play sentence. The English line says what it should mean.
  2. Tap any word to hear just that word. If it is said wrong, press Said wrong and type how it should sound, in English letters, the way you would text it. Long vowels: type them double (aa, ee, oo) or use the letter buttons.
  3. Tick what was wrong with it. Words with a dotted underline are ones the computer guessed, so they are the most likely to be off.
  4. If the whole sentence has a problem (robotic, odd pauses, pitch), tick it under The sentence as a whole.
  5. Press Next. At the end press Copy results or Download results and give them to Claude. Everything is saved in this browser as you go.
