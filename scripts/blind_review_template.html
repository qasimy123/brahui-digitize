Brahui voice: blind comparison

24 sentences, each spoken by two versions of the voice model. You are not told which is which.

How to review
  1. Play A and B for a sentence (headphones help).
  2. Pick which one sounds like better, more natural Brahui. Tick any problems you hear.
  3. Add a note if a word is wrong or the accent sounds off. Notes in any language are fine.
  4. When finished, press Download results and tell Claude; the file lands in your Downloads folder.

The English lines are machine translations of sentence fragments and may be wrong; lines starting with "?" are the least certain. Correct them in the notes if you like.
