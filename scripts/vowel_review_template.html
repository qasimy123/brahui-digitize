Brahui word pronunciation check

No Arabic script needed. For each common word you hear real speakers say it, and see the spelling the computer voice will read. Fix the spelling when it doesn't match what you hear.

How to review
  • Press Word to hear the word alone, or Phrase to hear it inside a few words. The phrase is written out with the word in bold.
  • If the big spelling matches the sound, press Matches. If not, type it the way it sounds; it is then marked Fixed.
  • Spelling: a i u are short, ā ī ū are long (you can type aa ii uu), plus e and o. kh gh sh ch are single sounds; ṭ ḍ ṛ can be typed T D R.
  • The clips are cut by a machine, so some start late or catch a neighbouring word. Most words have two recordings: if one is wrong, ignore it and judge from the other. Press No clip is right only when none of them is the word shown. If you can't tell, press Unsure.
  • Words are ordered from least to most certain, and each shows how sure the computer is of its spelling. The first group matters most; the last group is mostly a quick confirmation.
  • You don't have to finish: answers save as you go. Press Download results when you stop and tell Claude.
